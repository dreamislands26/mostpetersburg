How Can Foreign Tourists Pay in Russia?

Payment methods in Russia for foreign visitors: cards, cash, currency exchange and planning before travel.

General planning information · Published November 2026. Rules, routes and costs may change. Check official providers before acting.

Will my foreign bank card work?

Do not assume a Visa or Mastercard issued outside Russia will work at Russian merchants or ATMs. Confirm your card issuer's current support and the applicable payment network restrictions before travel.

What about cash?

Check current customs rules, currency controls and your home country's restrictions before carrying or exchanging cash. Use authorised exchange services and keep documentation where required.

Can I book activities in advance?

Ask each provider which lawful payment methods are available to your nationality and payment provider. MOST Petersburg can discuss individual arrangements only after verifying a workable payment route.

A practical checklist

Confirm your payment options before departure, budget for local transport and attractions, and avoid relying on a single payment method.
