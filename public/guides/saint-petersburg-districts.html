Saint Petersburg Neighbourhoods & Districts

Explore the historic centre, Kolomna and Vasilyevsky Island with a local Saint Petersburg destination guide.

This guide draws on the MOST Petersburg visitor-guide archive and has been rewritten for contemporary planning. Operational details should always be independently reconfirmed.

Historic centre

Palace Square, the Winter Palace, Nevsky Prospekt and nearby waterways provide a strong introduction to the city.

Kolomna

Kolomna is known for its canals, layered architecture, proximity to the Mariinsky Theatre and quieter historic streets.

Vasilyevsky Island

The island includes the Strelka, Rostral Columns, embankments and long streets with distinct urban character.

Beyond the centre

Suburban palace ensembles and nearby historic towns can be incorporated into longer stays.

Planning your visit

Choose neighbourhoods by interest and available time rather than trying to cover every attraction in a single day.
