How Much Does a Trip to Saint Petersburg Cost?

How to plan a Saint Petersburg travel budget across hotels, museums, transport, meals and private tours.

General planning information · Published November 2026. Rules, routes and costs may change. Check official providers before acting.

Accommodation

Prices depend on season, hotel category, location and booking conditions. White Nights and major events may affect availability and rates.

Sightseeing and museums

Plan separately for museum tickets, specialist guides, transport and excursions outside the city, such as Peterhof or Tsarskoe Selo.

Meals and local transport

Allow for daily meals, airport transfers and local travel. Check accepted payment methods before departure.

Private tour pricing

Private itineraries are quoted for your dates, group size, guide language, included admissions and transport. We do not publish unverified fixed prices.
