How to Travel to Saint Petersburg, Russia

International travel planning for Russia: visas, routes, entry checks and Saint Petersburg arrival logistics.

General planning information · Published November 2026. Rules, routes and costs may change. Check official providers before acting.

Start with entry eligibility

Your nationality, passport, purpose of travel and intended dates determine the applicable visa or visa-free regime. Check official consular information before purchasing non-refundable travel.

Choose a viable route

Flight and overland connections change. Confirm airline operations, border checkpoint rules, transit visas and any travel advisories that apply to your journey.

Plan your arrival

Consider the distance from the airport or railway station, hotel check-in arrangements, mobile connectivity and payment options. Confirm transfers before departure.

Make the visit worthwhile

Pair a practical arrival plan with a curated museum, heritage or palace itinerary.
