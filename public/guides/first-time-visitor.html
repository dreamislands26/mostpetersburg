First-Time Visitor Guide to Saint Petersburg

Plan a first visit to Saint Petersburg with museums, cultural neighbourhoods, logistics and tailored itinerary ideas.

This guide draws on the MOST Petersburg visitor-guide archive and has been rewritten for contemporary planning. Operational details should always be independently reconfirmed.

Start with the essentials

The Hermitage, Palace Square, St Isaac's Cathedral and the historic waterways offer an introduction to the city's architecture and collections.

Add a neighbourhood

Balance major sights with Kolomna, Vasilyevsky Island or another area that reflects your interests.

Allow time for museums

Large collections can take several hours. Verify ticketing arrangements and opening days directly with venues.

Plan transport and entry

Entry requirements, international travel connections and accepted payment methods can change. Confirm them using official current sources before booking.

Planning your visit

MOST Petersburg can develop a tailored proposal; all service arrangements are subject to confirmation.
