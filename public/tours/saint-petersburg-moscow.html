From the MOST archive: Seven Days Across Two Capitals

An original MOST Petersburg autumn/winter programme from 2019–2020 provides a concrete example of a seven-day, Moscow-first cultural itinerary. The outline below reflects that historic programme, not a confirmed current package.

DAYS 01–02

Arrival & Moscow icons

Airport arrival and hotel transfer, followed by Red Square, GUM and Saint Basil's Cathedral.

DAY 03

Moscow Metro & Kremlin

Architectural exploration of the metro and a proposed Kremlin visit, subject to admission and opening arrangements.

DAY 04

Novodevichy & rail journey

A cultural visit to Novodevichy, then an intercity train to Saint Petersburg with onward hotel transfer.

DAY 05

Imperial Petersburg

Saint Isaac's Cathedral and a proposed Peter and Paul Fortress visit, with time for independent exploration.

DAY 06

Art & architecture

Hermitage Museum and Kazan Cathedral, subject to museum opening times and admission.

DAY 07

Departure

Hotel check-out and onward travel, with train and airport arrangements designed around the actual flight schedule.

Customising the journey

The historical programme offered different accommodation categories and private ground transport. A new itinerary can instead prioritise family pacing, museum interests, accessibility, language requirements or additional days in either city.

Source: MOST Petersburg's internal “Moscow & St. Petersburg — 7 Days — Autumn/Winter 2019–2020” itinerary. Old prices, train times, hotel selections and service terms are intentionally not carried forward.

Planning notes
