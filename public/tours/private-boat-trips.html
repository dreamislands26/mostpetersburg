From the MOST archive: Night Drawbridges by Private Boat

MOST's original boat-trip concept describes a two-hour evening journey timed around Saint Petersburg's drawbridge openings. The historic brief highlights views of Palace Bridge, Trinity Bridge and the former Lieutenant Schmidt Bridge (now Blagoveshchensky Bridge), with a departure point planned near the guest's hotel where feasible.

Suggested experience flow

  1. Agree a suitable embarkation point and meet the skipper or guide.
  2. Explore the central Neva waterfront and the city's illuminated architectural landmarks.
  3. Position for the drawbridge openings where navigation permissions and conditions allow.
  4. Return to an agreed pier; optional hotel transfers can be considered in the proposal.

What makes the bridges special?

Saint Petersburg's movable bridges enable river navigation along the Neva. Their openings have become a defining night-time spectacle, especially during the White Nights season. Opening schedules, routes and viewing positions vary; check current official bridge information before planning.

Historical MOST concept: approximately two hours, with optional hotel transfer. This is not a currently confirmed departure, schedule or price. Vessel, route, safety requirements and availability must be reconfirmed.

Planning notes

Waterway operations are seasonal and subject to weather, navigation restrictions and vessel availability.
