Our original four-day Scarlet Sails journey

MOST Petersburg developed a private four-day Scarlet Sails programme for 19–22 June 2020. It combined the festival atmosphere with major cultural highlights and personalised ground arrangements. The programme is presented here as an example of our historical product design, not a current bookable offer.

DAY 1

Arrival

Airport meet-and-greet and private hotel transfer.

DAY 2

Imperial city & festival

Peter and Paul Fortress, Hermitage and a planned evening Scarlet Sails viewing experience.

DAY 3

Peterhof & Fabergé

Imperial gardens and palace, followed by Fabergé Museum.

Day 4: Private departure transfer. The historical package also specified a licensed private guide, private vehicle, breakfast accommodation and dedicated trip coordination.

Historical 2020 brochure: indicative per-person prices €549–599 (3-star), €779–799 (4-star), €1,299–1,399 (5-star). These are archived 2020 quotations, not current rates, available hotels or 2027 offers.
