Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood

A striking landmark beside the Griboyedov Canal, and the starting point referenced in the historic MOST walk.

02

Mikhailovsky Garden

A green pause in the historic centre, close to the Russian Museum and the canal.

03

Mikhailovsky Castle

An imperial residence with a distinctive architectural history.

04

Panteleimonovsky Bridge

A historic bridge near the Fontanka and the Summer Garden area.

One of the city's best-known historic gardens, with avenues, sculpture and fountains.
