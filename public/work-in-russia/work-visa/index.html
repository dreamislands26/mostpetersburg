Russia Work Visa: A Starting Guide

Understand Russia work visa and employment authorisation basics, employer responsibilities and relocation planning.

General planning information · Published November 2026. Rules, routes and costs may change. Check official providers before acting.

Work status depends on citizenship

Rules differ for nationals of different countries, including possible exemptions and separate immigration pathways. Confirm your specific status before accepting employment.

Employer responsibilities

An employer may need to follow hiring, invitation, notification or work-authorisation procedures. The exact process depends on the worker's status and role.

Tourist entry is not employment permission

Do not use tourist entry or an electronic tourist visa as a substitute for the legal right to work.

Verify before relocating

Ask the prospective employer for a written offer and confirm the immigration pathway with official authorities or qualified counsel. MOST Petersburg does not offer verified jobs, recruitment or work permits.
