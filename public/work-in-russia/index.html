Employment requires the correct legal basis

Do not assume a tourist visa or electronic tourist visa permits employment. Verify the appropriate immigration and work-authorisation route with the employer and qualified advisers.

Employer-first process

Before relocating, obtain a verifiable job offer, written contract details and a clear explanation of sponsorship, remuneration and compliance responsibilities.

Relocation and local orientation

Housing searches, neighbourhood orientation, cultural information and local logistics may be arranged separately after the underlying employment and immigration route has been verified.

Current service status

MOST Petersburg is not advertising verified vacancies or promising job placement. Recruitment and immigration services would require appropriate partners and a separately validated legal framework.
