About MOST · Established 2018

You Are the
MOST Welcome.

Established in 2018, MOST Petersburg brings together destination storytelling and travel industry perspectives to introduce Saint Petersburg to international audiences.

Our brand promise

From inspiration to on-the-ground arrangements.

Our original slogan, “You Are the MOST Welcome,” expresses the hospitality at the heart of the brand. We believe a destination is more than a list of sights. Our work starts with a traveller’s interests, the city's seasons and cultural context, then develops into a thoughtful local journey. MOST Petersburg is an independent travel and destination partner, not an official tourism authority.

Our approach

Our destination work dates back to 2018. The MOST Petersburg archive includes destination guides, city maps, travel product concepts and editorial work spanning architecture, neighborhoods, culture and hospitality. The relaunched platform builds on this foundation while reviewing all historical content and partnerships for present-day relevance.
