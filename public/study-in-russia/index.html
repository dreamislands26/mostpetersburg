Choose a university and programme

Compare language of instruction, academic level, entry examinations, tuition and university-specific application deadlines. Some programmes are offered in English; many are taught in Russian.

Apply through official channels

Universities publish their own admissions requirements. Official resources include Study in Russia and Education in Russia for government-funded quota pathways.

Student visa and arrival

If a student visa is required, follow the university's invitation and consular process. Tourist e-visas are not substitutes for student status.

Local orientation

MOST Petersburg can discuss city orientation, neighbourhoods, cultural introductions and arrival logistics, subject to a separately confirmed proposal. We are not currently an authorised university admissions representative.
