Universities in Saint Petersburg for International Students

A guide to universities in Saint Petersburg for international students, admissions pathways and arrival planning.

General planning information · Published November 2026. Rules, routes and costs may change. Check official providers before acting.

Explore institutions

Saint Petersburg State University (SPbU), ITMO University, Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University and Pavlov First Saint Petersburg State Medical University are among the institutions prospective students may research.

Compare programmes

Check each university's official catalogue for degree availability, language of instruction, admission exams, tuition, deadlines and recognition requirements.

Scholarships and quotas

Government-funded places and scholarship routes have their own application rules. Consult official admissions and quota platforms rather than third-party guarantees.

Plan your move

Once admitted, confirm the correct visa and registration procedures with your university. MOST Petersburg may discuss city orientation and cultural experiences, but does not claim admissions partnerships.
