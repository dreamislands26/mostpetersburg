Russian unified e-visa

For eligible nationalities, Russia's unified electronic visa is single-entry, valid for 120 days after issue and permits a stay of up to 30 days. The arrival and departure days both count. Check eligibility and entry points with the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs before applying.

Application timing

The official application window is no earlier than 86 days and no later than four days before planned entry. Applications are submitted through the official MFA website.
