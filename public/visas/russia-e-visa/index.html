Russia E-Visa: What Travellers Need to Know

Check official requirements before planning your journey. Rules may change and eligibility depends on nationality.

Information reviewed 10 November 2026. Verify changing rules with the relevant official authority.

Validity and stay

The unified e-visa is single-entry. Its validity is 120 days from issue, and the permitted stay is no more than 30 days from entry, within the visa's validity.

What is needed

The official process requires passport details and digital photographs. For this visa category, the MFA states that an invitation letter or hotel booking confirmation is not required.

Permitted travel purpose

The official scheme covers tourism and certain private, business, cultural and other specified visits. It is not a general work or study visa.

Plan the arrival

Confirm the authorised border checkpoint, route, travel documents and payment arrangements before departure.
