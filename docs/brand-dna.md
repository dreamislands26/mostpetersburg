# MOST Petersburg — Brand DNA v1.0
Updated: 2026-11-10

## Identity
**MOST Petersburg — An Independent Destination Experience & Travel Partner.**
**Brand line:** Saint Petersburg. Beyond the Ordinary.
**Founded:** 2018. Independent; never describe MOST as an official tourism board, immigration authority, university agent or event organiser.

## Purpose
Make Saint Petersburg easier to discover, understand and experience through local knowledge, culturally rich journeys and thoughtful destination coordination.

## Promise
From first inspiration to on-the-ground arrangements: a locally informed, personalised experience with clear scope, responsible claims and verified suppliers.

## Brand pillars — adapted from global DMO practice
1. **NYC / Seasonal demand creation:** Saint Petersburg Seasons: Winter Culture, White Nights, Autumn Arts. Each season = editorial hub + itineraries + partner offers + measurable inquiry conversion.
2. **Singapore / Audience-first storytelling:** Serve distinct traveller needs, not a generic city catalogue. Cultural Explorers; Private & Premium Travellers; Event Travellers; Business Delegations; Long-Stay Arrivals; Travel Trade.
3. **LA / Event-led destination hubs:** Major events are discovery hooks; connect programme, travel planning, cultural experiences, logistics and qualified inquiries. Never promise organiser access.
4. **Partnership network:** Develop mutually beneficial relationships with guides, hotels, cultural venues, transport operators and travel advisors. Verify active relationships before public claims.
5. **Destination intelligence:** Useful guides, honest seasonal planning, updated official links, international audience context.
6. **Measurable conversion:** Track impressions → qualified visits → brief submissions → proposals → confirmed bookings → gross profit.

## Customer-facing architecture
- **Discover:** /guides/, neighbourhoods, practical arrival advice.
- **Experience:** /private-tours/, /luxury-travel/, /events/, /tours/.
- **Plan:** /visas/, practical guides, seasonality.
- **Partner:** /travel-trade.html, /dmc-russia/, /destination-marketing.html.
- **Convert:** a single clear request-for-proposal journey; do not imply the current form is operational until backend delivery is verified.

## Signature seasonal concepts
- **Winter Culture:** museums, theatre, culinary heritage, festive city.
- **White Nights:** evening city, canals, imperial heritage, summer cultural programmes.
- **Autumn Arts:** literature, performing arts, architecture and neighbourhood discovery.
No fixed tickets, event dates, prices or guaranteed access without supplier confirmation.

## Voice and visual rules
Editorial, precise, inviting, locally knowledgeable. Premium without empty luxury superlatives. Cultural depth over tourist clichés. Deep slate / warm ivory / muted gold; generous spacing, strong editorial photography with rights cleared. Avoid official-looking insignia, unverifiable awards, fake ratings, artificial scarcity or unverified testimonials.

## Conversion and compliance
Primary CTA: **Plan Your Petersburg Journey** (B2C), **Send a Travel Trade Brief** (B2B).
Qualify by dates, party size, interests, language, budget range and contact preference; collect only necessary personal data with privacy notice. Distinguish proposals from confirmed bookings. Visa, education and employment content is informational unless independently authorised partners and services are established.

## KPI and editorial governance
Weekly: Search Console impressions, non-brand clicks, indexed pages, top countries, inquiries and conversion.
Monthly: qualified leads, proposals, bookings, gross margin, partner contribution, season and segment performance.
Event content: show 'confirmed by organiser' vs 'seasonal planning window', source, review date; archive past-year claims. Localise based on evidence, not assumption.

## Internal decision test
Every new page or campaign should answer:
1. Which traveller segment and intent?
2. Why visit now?
3. What distinctive local experience or insight?
4. Which credible source or confirmed partner supports it?
5. What is the next action and how will it be measured?
