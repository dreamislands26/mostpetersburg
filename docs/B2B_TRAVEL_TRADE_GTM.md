# MOST Petersburg — B2B Travel Trade GTM / LOB Roadmap
Date: 2026-11-11
Source: user-provided GTM framework and critique. Planning assumptions are not operational guarantees.

## Positioning
Independent Saint Petersburg destination partner for international agencies and corporate planners. Brand promise: “You Are the MOST Welcome.” Distinguish destination editorial from trade conversion.

## Lines of Business
| LOB | ICP | Offer | Primary conversion |
| Luxury & Bespoke FIT | Luxury travel designers, concierges | Custom cultural itineraries, private guiding, curated experiences | Request a bespoke quote |
| MICE & Corporate | Corporate travel managers, event agencies | Incentives, meetings, event logistics | Submit event brief |
| Groups & Series | Outbound operators, wholesalers | Group itineraries, Moscow–Petersburg combinations | Request group rates |
| DMC & Ground Handling | Agencies needing local execution | Transfers, guides, coordination, logistics | Become a trade partner |

## Stage 1 — Foundations (Weeks 1–3)
- Validate suppliers, actual services, legal contracting entity, cancellation policy, payments and response SLAs.
- Create LOB offer sheets, inclusions/exclusions, pricing rules, net rates vs commissions, language capability and markets.
- Publish /travel-trade/ hub and /travel-trade/luxury-fit/, /mice/, /groups/, /ground-handling/.
- Each page: explicit ICP, capabilities, sample itinerary, evidence, FAQ, brief form and conversion event.
- No unsupported guarantees: hotel allotments, private palace openings, visa issuance, 24/7 support, currencies or payment rails.

## Stage 2 — Sales Enablement (Weeks 2–5)
- Create 4 one-page capabilities sheets and sample quote templates.
- Build partner intake form: agency/company, country, email, LOB, dates, group size, language, budget, requirements, consent.
- Define CRM stages: Target → Contacted → Qualified → Brief Received → Quote Sent → Negotiation → Won/Lost → Repeat.
- Segment an initial 100 vetted accounts by source market and LOB; verify contact accuracy and lawful outreach.
- Build objection-handling cards: visa/entry, payment, logistics, operational scope, cancellation.

## Stage 3 — GTM Planning (Weeks 4–8)
- Search intent: St Petersburg DMC, Russia inbound ground handler, luxury Russia travel designer, Russia MICE partner, St Petersburg group tours.
- Create English first; prioritize zh-CN and Arabic only with verified localized sales/operations.
- Outreach: personalized agency email + LinkedIn/WeChat/WhatsApp where appropriate; avoid bulk unsolicited messaging.
- Build seasonal offers (White Nights, winter cultural programs) with actual supplier availability.
- Publish trade case studies only with permission and evidence.

## Stage 4 — Execution & Feedback (Weeks 6–13)
- Weekly funnel review: visits → qualified inquiries → quotes → bookings → gross margin → repeat.
- Diagnose lost deals by source market, LOB, payment friction and turnaround time.
- Improve highest-converting LOB pages and quote workflows.
- Target 24h first response as a proposed internal SLA, not an external promise until resourced.

## Measurement
- GA4 events: view_trade_hub, select_lob, start_trade_brief, submit_trade_brief, request_rates, download_trade_kit.
- Capture UTM source/medium/campaign and LOB, never sensitive personal data in analytics.
- Track qualified lead rate, quote rate, quote-to-booking, revenue, gross margin, response time, repeat agencies.
- Initial goals are hypotheses, not reported performance.

## Web UX corrections
- Homepage navigation: Discover, Experiences, What's on, Plan your visit, For partners.
- Homepage: one editorial promise, real photography, clear Discover CTA; trade section should be a concise gateway, not a payment-process essay.
- Remove public-facing Stripe mechanics from storytelling pages; payment details belong in quotes/checkout.
- Replace vague copy with concrete route/venue/itinerary information.
- Fix duplicate section numbering, add spring season or remove exhaustive seasonal claim.
- Preserve slogan as deliberate MOST wordplay; explain brand positioning without presenting it as standard grammar.
- Replace the 2022 map boast with an accurately dated archival feature, separate from current verified visit information.

## P0 acceptance
- Every LOB page has meaningful, non-fabricated offer content and a working lead capture route.
- Each form is tested end-to-end, with consent, spam prevention and notification ownership.
- Analytics conversions are tested; no claim of live lead capture before confirmation.
- Production deployment and mobile/desktop rendering checked independently of GitHub commits.
